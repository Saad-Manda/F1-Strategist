Phase 9 · Page 08 · The comparison

“Same Timesteps” Isn't “Same Experience”

The plan's fairness protocol, and the arithmetic that contradicts it.

Your call · two budgets

The plan's DQN trains 5,000 episodes of a 52-lap race. Its configs/ppo.yaml sets total_timesteps: 2000000, and its protocol says “same total environment steps, ~2M for both”.

How much more experience does PPO get?

The arithmetic

DQN: 5,000 episodes × 52 laps ≈ 260,000 steps PPO: total_timesteps = 2,000,000 steps 2,000,000 / 260,000 ≈ 7.7

The config's own comment compounds it: “~2M steps; equivalent to ~5000 episodes”, but 2,000,000 / 52 ≈ 38,500 episodes. Off by the same 7.7: two numbers meant to agree that quietly didn't.

The reveal · two budgets

The numbers sayAbout 8 times

PPO gets 7.7 times DQN's experience. Any “PPO beat DQN”, or the reverse, then mixes the algorithm with the budget. The Definition of Done's “100 paired seeds” controls evaluation luck (Phase 3's discipline), not this.

Lab p9-08 measures it on the toy race: a table Q-learner read at its own final point and the mini PPO at its far larger one differ by 24.6 s; read over the same window of steps, by 5.8 s. About three quarters of the first answer was the budget.

Two fixes that are principled

Not “give DQN 2M steps” on its own, which silently changes Phase 5's tuned budget. Either:

  • Match the budgets before training, and say in the write-up what number was chosen and why.
  • Train each to its own budget and compare at matched steps, on a shared environment-step axis, as the plan's comparison notebook already intends. Sample efficiency is a real difference between an off-policy and an on-policy learner; made visible, the gap is a finding instead of a confound.

What the comparison would say about the case

Put J3 into this table. On the toy race the raw MaskablePPO ends 38.48 s from the optimum after 2,000 races, while Phase 5's network DQN learner, centred, reached 0.00 to 4.3 s in 600. A comparison run with an uncentred PPO would conclude “DQN wins by 34 to 38 seconds”, pass every item of its checklist, and be about the reward, not the algorithm. With J2 left in too, it would also have given the loser eight times the data.

Both learners are re-plotted against environment steps instead of episodes. Can that change which curve is ahead at a given point?
Worked solution for the warm-up

171,000 steps against 1,500,000: a factor of 8.77, and 1,500,000 steps is about 26,316 races. With fixed-length episodes a step axis is a rescaled episode axis, so the choice of axis cannot reorder the curves. Only reading both at the same budget can.
