Why does the stop's timing matter now?

3 · What it means

With the line in place a pit stop does two things: it costs 20 s, and it gets you out of traffic. That second job, which did not exist a moment ago, is what an undercut uses.

In a race without the blocking line, what can a successful overtake change?
Worked solution

No blocking: 30 × 0.5 − 20 = −5, so FAST finishes 5.0 s behind for any pit tick. Blocking, level start: FAST is held to SLOW's pace from tick 0 until its stop, so the gap is 5 + 0.5 × tick: 7.5 at tick 5, 17.5 at tick 25, smallest (5.0) at tick 0. A pass with no blocking has nothing to release a car from.
