Check the claimed interaction before optimizing

Phase 8’s proposed interaction goal inherits Phase 6’s physical traffic semantics. A faster follower held to a slower leader’s resolved pace has something that a pass or pit-timing intervention can release. Sorting independent times alone does not represent that traffic resource. It can still produce timing patterns through tyres or weather, so blocking is a dependency of this particular strategic mechanism, not a necessary condition for every two-peak histogram.

Use the same fresh pre-step times [0,.5] and paces [5,4.5]. No cap gives [5,5]; cap with forced failure gives [5,5⅚], preserving a positive gap; forced release permits [5,5]. Multi-step schedules and pit effects must then be traced explicitly. One intervention establishes this toy’s consequence, not that a learner will discover undercut/overcut policies.

The legacy toy timing bonus rewards PAPER while trailing, but its learner observes only one shared action-value table. It cannot represent separate trailing/leading policies unless the state is extended. Correlation with trailing also reflects its own past actions; shuffling flags arbitrarily does not automatically yield a valid significance test. Preserve the diagnostic idea while avoiding a promised effect from an unrepresentable state.
