At 52 decisions per race, 200,000 decisions is about 3846 races; at 1000 retained transitions and one update per four thereafter, 4000 total transitions yield 751 updates if the first update is allowed exactly at transition 1000. Trace collection before any replay update

At reset, obtain observation and current action mask. Select among legal actions using the behavior epsilon. Step once and snapshot the actual successor observation, reward, both flags and successor mask. Increment the environment counter once, insert the transition, and update only when warm-up and cadence permit. Stop collection on either flag. Autoreset requires saving the final observation/mask explicitly instead of accidentally storing the next episode's start.

Batch states and successors become [B,16], actions long[B], rewards float[B], termination bool[B], and next masks bool[B,6]. With a plain or dueling network and the independent target, the earlier dqn_step supplies the complete optimizer operation. Use double=True for online selection/target evaluation, huber=True for the planned smooth-L1 loss, and detached PER weights when enabled. Its returned errors are pre-update, unweighted and detached.

Compose the mechanisms without hiding differences

For a controlled ablation, explicitly choose plain versus dueling representation, vanilla versus Double target, and uniform versus prioritized replay. These are proposed integration switches, not existing switches promised by the supplied production plan. Uniform replay uses unit weights; prioritized replay supplies its documented correction and feeds returned errors back through raw-to-mass conversion. Both retain the same masks and ending rules. Synchronize target parameters after the successful optimizer step.

The plan's sum tree stores powered leaf masses, but its default insertion reads their maximum and applies alpha again. If maximum raw priority is 9 with alpha 0.5, the largest mass is 3; powering it again inserts sqrt(3), not 3. Correct this boundary by tracking a maximum raw priority and powering once, or by passing a clearly named mass directly to a mass-storing leaf. Its sampled weights also normalize within the minibatch, whereas the pack normalizes over retained entries. Do not call them the same normalization or compare the pack's exact weight witness against that different implementation. Stratified sampling, beta counted in sampling calls, and soft target updates must each be recorded.

Save and evaluate the intended learner

For inference save online weights, architecture, feature/action order, reward transform and configuration. For faithful resume also save target weights, optimizer history, counters, replay contents/priorities and random states. The plan's simple weights-only-style checkpoint does not establish a fully reproducible training resume. Validate compatibility before loading.

Evaluation sets exploration to zero, uses eval() and no_grad, and performs no replay insertion, optimization, counter decay or synchronization. Zero epsilon can still involve an explicit tie convention. Keep evaluation environments/random streams separate from training. Collect physical time, penalties and oracle-relative gaps under aligned weather/SC information; never let a future-known oracle's information advantage masquerade as a pure learning gap.

Recap and self-check

Why is an environment counter incremented only by decay_epsilon() fragile?

Calling update before decay versus after it changes cadence. Count transitions once in the collector and document whether synchronization and beta advance on optimizer updates or sampling calls.
