Trace the planned interface with lifecycle corrections

The Phase 8 plan proposes F1PettingZooEnv with per-agent 19-feature observations and six-action masks, and train_self_play with learner agent_0 against sampled frozen opponents. The 19-feature proposal inherits the gap/sentinel and hidden-state limitations. It is a contract to implement, not an existing certified multi-car API.

Collect all live agents’ actions from the same pre-transition snapshot, validate their IDs/masks, resolve current-lap interaction costs together, accumulate each physical time once, derive ranks and per-agent rewards, then expose successor observations and flags. The sketch creates independent F1StrategyEnv instances; its reward-only dirty air and sorted times do not automatically inherit Phase 6’s corrected blocking dynamics. Shared scenario realizations and joint resolution must actually be connected.

Its live-agent filter removes true terminations only, leaving truncated agents active. Remove either ending condition for subsequent actions while retaining each final transition for replay. Do not silently substitute missing rewards or reset observations with default zeros. If invalid actions are replaced with a fallback, record the executed action for learning; preferably reject invalid learner actions at the boundary so policy and transition labels cannot disagree.

Pool and trainer ownership

Plan settings are max_pool_size 50, recency_bias .7 and checkpoint_freq 1000 episodes. Only learner replay and weights update; frozen opponents do not optimize. Save independent models with feature/action metadata and explicit seed-policy types. Cache immutable loaded opponents and reuse resettable environments to avoid repeatedly reading calibration/scenario files across a proposed 20,000-episode run. These are performance options after correctness, not promised speedups.

The plan’s learner store must preserve current and final successor masks, both ending flags, actual actions and opponent IDs. Decay schedules count the intended learner decisions, not arbitrary calls. Report joint and learner transition budgets separately.

Recap and self-check

Does wrapping independent single-car environments make a shared race?

No. Shared conditions, simultaneous physical interactions and consistent reward/time accounting must be implemented and tested.
