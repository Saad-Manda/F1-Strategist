3 · Oracle A or Oracle B?

How is the oracle's race time obtained for a results table?
Worked solution

1. 70 × 5 × 70 × 8 × 2 × 3 = 1,176,000. Divided by 726,000 that is 1.62, which is (70 / 55)².

2. Unreachable: tyre older than the race so far; fitted compound missing from the used set; a never-changed tyre with two compounds used. Reachable: the lap-40 state and the start state.

3. B, A, B, A. The oracle is scored by rolling its policy out in the real environment.
