Separate mechanical and learning failures

A high score with twice-counted rivals is an accounting failure; an overtake discarded by sorting is a model failure; weak performance with correct reachable dynamics may be a learning failure. Test the first two independently. Competent fixed rivals are baselines, not proofs of equilibrium or broad robustness.
