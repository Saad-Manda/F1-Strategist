Phase 6 · Page 03 · Potential-based shaping

Potential-Based Shaping

Ng, Harada & Russell (1999) found the one form a shaping bonus can take without being able to change what's best.

Your call · a shaped chain

From A, either go via B to the end C (rewards −3 then −1) or straight to C (−10). Add a bonus to each step: the potential of where you land minus the potential of where you left, with Φ(A) = 5, Φ(B) = 2, Φ(C) = 0.

What does the bonus add to each route's total?

Ad-hoc shaping can change the answer

Nothing stops a designer adding any extra term to a reward to speed learning up. Nothing guarantees it leaves the best policy alone either. Add a flat bonus for any pit stop, meant to encourage trying stops early in training. Make it large enough and pitting looks good at any tyre age: the shaped optimum becomes "pit constantly", a different and worse policy. Careless shaping can teach the wrong thing outright.

The theorem

Take any function of the state alone, Φ(s), a potential, and add the discounted difference in potential to every step's reward:

F(s, a, s′) = γ·Φ(s′) − Φ(s)

Then, whatever Φ is, the best policy under the shaped reward is the best policy under the unshaped one. Only how fast it is found can change. The reason in one line: for every policy, Q′(s, a) = Q(s, a) − Φ(s). Every call at a state shifts by the same amount, and a shift that is the same for every call can't change which call is largest.

The numbers say−5 to both

Via B: F(A,B) = 2 − 5 = −3 and F(B,C) = 0 − 2 = −2, so the route goes from −4 to −9. Direct: F(A,C) = 0 − 5 = −5, from −10 to −15. Both shift by −5 = Φ(C) − Φ(A), because the middle potential is added on one step and taken back on the next. The gap between the routes stays 6, and via B still wins.

The steps' own rewards never enter the shift. Only the start and the end do, which is where the one condition comes from.

The one condition: every ending has the same potential

Over a whole episode with γ = 1 the bonuses add up to Φ(end) − Φ(start). Every route shares the start. They share the end only if every way of ending has the same potential. Give the direct route its own ending C′ with Φ(C′) = 8: its shaped total becomes −10 + 3 = −7, which now beats −9. Every term was still a potential difference, and the ranking flipped. A potential left at the end is a bonus for ending there. So every state where an episode can end gets the same potential, conventionally 0: in code, Φ(s′) = 0 on the step that ends the race.

The plan's Φ

The plan's potential_shaping design names Φ(s) = −position / n_cars: a better place is a better potential. Two conditions make the theorem cover it. The code must compute a real difference γ·Φ(s′) − Φ(s), and the plan's code does not (page 06, E5). And Φ must be 0 on the final step, which the plan doesn't say: kept at the finish, the leftover potential pays 1/n_cars per place gained by the end, a hidden finishing bonus. Even with E5 fixed, its Definition-of-Done test, that the shaped and unshaped optima agree, isn't guaranteed.

Deep diveThe algebra behind Q′ = Q − Φ120 words

Take any policy π and guess V′(s) = V(s) − Φ(s). Put it into the Bellman equation with the shaped reward:

Q′(s,a) = E[ r + γ·Φ(s′) − Φ(s) + γ·(V(s′) − Φ(s′)) ] = E[ r + γ·V(s′) ] − Φ(s) (the γ·Φ(s′) terms cancel) = Q(s,a) − Φ(s)

The guess is consistent, state by state, for every policy, the best one included, so argmax_a Q′*(s,a) = argmax_a Q*(s,a). At a terminal state there is no next value, V = 0 under both rewards, and the guess holds there only if Φ(terminal) = 0: the algebra's version of the condition above. It is the move Phase 5 page 08 used for centring, per state instead of per race.

Deep diveThe four designs on the plan's race130 words

On the Pit Wall's race with blocking off, the place bonus, gap_change, the potential with its zero at the finish, the potential without it, and the plan's flat bonus (page 06, E5) all put the lap-8 stop first. The flat bonus pays 0.5 for every lap you are ahead, so it moves lap 9 up to second, ahead of lap 7: a stop on lap 9 is ahead for 19 laps, lap 8 for 18. None of them can move the top. With blocking on, the place bonus, gap_change and all three shapings put lap 6 first, and lap time alone has laps 6 and 7 level to within 0.01 s.
