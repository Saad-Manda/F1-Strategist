Choose the learning organization explicitly

Independent learners each maintain their own policy/value and experience, treating others as part of their effective environment. Parameter sharing pools experience through one network, with role or identity inputs when needed; symmetry alone does not guarantee faster learning. Centralized training with decentralized execution lets a training critic see joint information while actors use only allowed local observations at deployment.

The Phase 8 plan actually updates agent_0 against frozen historical opponents sampled per episode. This is a single changing learner versus a pool, rather than every car optimizing concurrently. It uses local observations and no centralized critic. Record that distinction when describing nonstationarity and comparing methods.

Fictitious play responds to an empirical distribution of opponent strategies. Sampling a snapshot at episode start produces a mixture over whole policies. Averaging weight tensors generally produces another function, not that same mixture. Even averaging each state’s action probabilities need not reproduce the episode-level correlations of selecting one coherent strategy for a whole race.

Robustness needs coverage

Compare uniform, recent-only and history-weighted training distributions on a fixed held-out opponent set. The matrix toy’s uniform policy has [0,0,0] coverage; a pure best response has a win, tie and loss. A population can improve coverage yet retain exploitable gaps. No pool variant is required to win every seed; mechanism checks and empirical performance are separate.

Recap and self-check

Are averaged network weights an empirical strategy mixture?

No. Sample a named policy or explicitly define action-level mixing; those procedures also have different trajectory correlations.
