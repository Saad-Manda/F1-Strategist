Case 5 · the verdict

Position was free, so timing was worthless

Why does stopping before the rival never gain anything? Because in the plan's race nothing holds a car behind the one ahead. Each car drives its own lap time, position is a sort of total times, and the rival's stop lap appears in none of your lap times. Your best stop is lap 8 against a rival stopping anywhere from lap 4 to 15, and every reward design in the plan agrees. The reward wasn't at fault and the race wasn't too small: the holding-up rule is missing, and two bugs (E2, E3) would keep position free even with it. Add the rule and the undercut is worth 3.83 s against a lap-9 rival, and your best stop follows the rival's.

What should the plan change? The prerequisites don't edit the plan; these go to whoever implements Phase 6.

1. Make position cost something. Add page 05's line to f1_multi_env.py's lap-time computation, before accumulation. Put the wake in the lap time (E3), make a pass change time rather than a position counter (E2), and judge a pass on this lap's times (E6a).

2. Test the consequence, not the parts. Keep the stuck-car test, and add one for the case: against a rival stopping on lap 9 and on lap 12, the best stop lap must differ. In a race without position it never does.

3. Fix the bookkeeping and the reward. Count each rival lap once (E1), keep agent and rivals on one lap index (E6b), add is_leader (E6c), let rivals stop under the safety car with the same discount (E6d), bring rival.py up to Phase 1's revised signature, and compute a real Φ difference with Φ = 0 at the finish (E5).

Also for Phase 8: its Risks table blames a missing undercut on the out-lap warmup penalty. The cause is here.
