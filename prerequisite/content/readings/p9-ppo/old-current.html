Keep the collection policy’s likelihood fixed

PPO collects a rollout with one policy version, storing actions, old selected log probabilities, old values, masks, rewards and boundaries. Compute advantages/return targets from that frozen rollout. Across optimization epochs, reevaluate current likelihoods on the same stored observations/actions/masks; do not resample actions or replace old logs after each update.

Old logs require detach and independent storage. detach() alone removes the gradient path but shares storage with the original; clone() alone copies while retaining a gradient path. The pack’s frozen returns detach().clone(). Mutating the source afterward cannot change the denominator and backward cannot reach the collection graph.

Suppose old action probability .5 becomes .65. The ratio is 1.3 throughout comparisons to that collecting policy. Replacing the denominator by .65 would reset it to 1 and erase the observed drift. New collection starts a new old-policy version; unlimited reuse of one old batch is not justified by clipping.

Values and masks are rollout data too

Recomputing critic advantages after every actor update changes the surrogate weights. Recomputing legal masks from a mutated environment instead of stored states changes support. Freeze those inputs and document normalization conventions. Current critic predictions remain trainable against the frozen targets.

def frozen(log_probs):
    vectors(log_probs)
    return log_probs.detach().clone()
