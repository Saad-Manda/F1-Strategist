Case 7 · the verdict

The algorithm was fine; its critic was asked to walk too far

Why does PPO learn nothing? With γ = 1 and the raw reward, the critic's targets are whole-race returns near −263 on the toy race (about −4,700 on the real one), and SB3's critic is a network trained by Adam, whose steps don't grow with the error: 9.9 every 10,000 steps here, centred or raw. Until the critic is in range, the advantages are its error, the same for every call, and the policy doesn't move. The clip never binds, nothing crashes, and the run ends on the plan that never chooses to stop: 38.48 s at 2,000 races, within 1 s only after 130,000 steps. The same algorithm with a table critic learned from the same raw reward, which is why the fault is the scale, not PPO.

What should the plan change? The prerequisites don't edit the plan; these go to whoever implements Phase 9.

1. Fix the scale. Train on the env with reward_offset: "base_pace" (Finding C), or wrap it in VecNormalize(norm_reward=True), the plan's own fallback. Both measured: 0.00 s on three seeds by 2,000 races. Say in the write-up which one, as the audit asks.

2. Make the checklist able to fail. Add an item a learner that learns nothing can't pass: train/explained_variance well above 0 within the first updates, and a PPO optimality gap below a stated threshold before the comparison runs.

3. Make the comparison fair. Match the training budgets or compare at matched steps (J2), and turn ε off when evaluating the DQN agents (D).

Also: DummyVecEnv, or the __main__ guard if SubprocVecEnv stays (J1), and gae_lambda written down.
