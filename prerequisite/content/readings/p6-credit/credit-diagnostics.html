Compare reward designs on shared physical outcomes

The proposed Phase 6 comparison has lap_time_plus_position, gap_change and potential_shaping. Before learning, enumerate a small frozen scenario’s routes under each definition. Keep both transformed reward and physical time, position, penalties and tyre decisions. Compare training runs on held-out scenarios with common opponent scripts and interaction budgets; a larger transformed reward across different definitions is not a meaningful winner.

For RISKY/SAFE, the true scores −10/−4 and proxy +6/+1 disagree. A zero-terminal potential with initial value 2 shifts both true returns to −12/−6, keeping SAFE best. This exact enumeration establishes the reward relationship on the toy without requiring 3000 episodes of Q-learning. It does not establish that a neural learner reaches either optimum.

A diagnostic intervention changes one action while freezing pre-step state and other actions. Record actual joint actions, seeds or event tapes, action masks, physical cost components and reward terms. Compare E1 exact-once accounting and E3 physical dirty-air application before treating a high score as competent behavior.

Use evidence at the right level

Correlation between early pitting and good finishes can reflect tyre condition, weather or initial position. A simulator intervention can test the mechanism under selected conditions; a held-out evaluation tests generalization under a protocol. Neither turns a tiny illustrative contribution into a universal causal statement. Training comparison is future work; the course’s browser and Python checks establish calculation and lifecycle contracts.
