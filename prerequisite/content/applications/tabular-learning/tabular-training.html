Every episode boundary clears transient behavior

Reset the environment and the agent's pending-action memory at the start of every training and evaluation race. True terminal targets use reward alone. External truncations can bootstrap from the final unfinished observation, but must not carry a pending action into the reset episode. An interrupted collector also needs explicit cleanup. Resetting pending behavior must not clear learned Q or unrelated learner settings.

The recorded pre-commit bug was a missing boundary reset, not a reason to conclude that every properly implemented terminal SARSA loop leaks an action. Test with a deliberately interrupted or truncated episode where a pending choice would otherwise survive, then verify that reset removes it. Test that a committed choice uses no additional policy draw when consumed.

A curriculum deliberately changes the sampled task

The early curriculum restricts weather to dry traces and suppresses stochastic SC starts. Both switches must be honored: filtering historical weather alone does not suppress freshly sampled interruptions. The plan's reset option such as curriculum_dry_only should document whether it enables both. Verify the environment behavior rather than merely checking that the option dictionary was constructed.

Initially holding weather/SC constant concentrates visits on fewer keys; later enable the full scenario and interruption model. Record the episode and decision count at the switch. Retain the same encoder/action/reward contract across stages, or explicitly version a changed task. A warm-started table can help, but dry success is not evidence of wet or SC performance.

Separate evaluation from exploratory training

The recorded evaluation bug left epsilon at its training floor 0.05. In a 52-step race, random-branch count has expectation 2.6; not every random-branch action differs from the greedy choice. Expensive exploratory pits can still distort a reported greedy-policy score. Force evaluation mode, freeze Q and counters, clear pending actions, and declare deterministic or seeded tie-breaking.

Periodic evaluation at a configured eval_interval can show progress, alongside the last-100 training-reward average. They measure different policies and can use different reward units: label raw versus centered scores. Repeated validation is for selection; keep a final held-out scenario/seed set for reporting. The same environment and policy seeds plus unchanged settings should reproduce trajectories under the declared generator and simulator.

Measure the meaningful gap

Report physical race time, constraint violations and agent_time−Oracle_B_time under matched weather law and paired scenarios. If an agent averages 5040 seconds and B 5000, its gap is 40 seconds. A baseline at 5100 leaves 100 seconds to close; this agent closes 60, or 60% of that baseline gap. Report uncertainty in the paired differences rather than calling that fraction exact.

A criterion such as beating a threshold baseline on at least 60 of 100 seeds is a project acceptance target, not a mathematical convergence proof. Beating random is only a weak integration check. Passing either while retaining a large oracle gap calls for inspecting representation, coverage, modeling assumptions and policy execution rather than declaring the method optimal.
