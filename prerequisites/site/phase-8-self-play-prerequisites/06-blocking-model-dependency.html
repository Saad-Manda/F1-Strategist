The Blocking Rule Everything Here Depends On

Restated from Phase 6, not re-derived — this is the point in the reading order where it stops being background and starts being the reason the rest of this phase can fail.

If you've read Phase 6 page 05, the summary below is the whole of what's new here

Nothing in this page's argument is original to Phase 8 — it's a direct consequence of Phase 6 page 05 (assumed background), restated at the exact point it becomes load-bearing for this phase's own headline result, per the same discipline Phase 6's own set used for restating Phase 1 material rather than re-deriving it.

The one-paragraph restatement

Phase 6's plan-as-written computes every car's race position purely by sorting cumulative total_time, ascending — there is no constraint anywhere in the dynamics that a car following closely behind another is ever actually held up by it. Under that model, pitting before a rival gains exactly as much track position as pitting whenever you simply happen to be faster: there is nothing for "the timing of a pit stop relative to a rival's" to add on top of "being faster," because being ahead or behind on track never costs or saves any time purely by virtue of position. An undercut and "being faster" collapse into the same event.

The mechanical consequence for self-play, spelled out

Train 20,000 self-play episodes against a position model with no blocking constraint, and there is structurally no reward signal that could ever teach either learning agent that pitting before a rival — rather than merely pitting whenever faster — has any distinct value of its own. The real plan's headline Definition of Done — a bimodal pit-timing histogram, with peaks at small negative (undercut) and small positive (overcut) deltas relative to a rival's pit lap — cannot appear, no matter how well the self-play mechanics (pages 01–05) otherwise converge, because the simulated world contains no mechanism whose presence such a histogram could even be evidence of.

A misdiagnosis worth naming directly

The real plan's Risks table now says this itself. Its "Undercut doesn't emerge" row reads: the out-lap warm-up penalty is not the likely cause; check the blocking model first. Two tempting fixes are still worth naming as misdiagnoses, because they are the ones a tired reader reaches for. More frequent checkpoints (the plan's remedy for a different row, "training collapse") and better recency tuning (page 03) address non-stationarity: they change how good an opponent's learned policy is, not what the environment's dynamics make possible for either agent to learn in the first place. Tuning the out-lap warm-up changes how big an undercut would be, not whether one can exist. In the capstone's duel (page 08) the warm-up is part of the tyre model; across the 12 tyre settings tried in the build, blocking off gave a pure equilibrium with every pit delta 0 in every one. Neither fix touches the actual gap: a reward signal for "timing your pit relative to a rival" that does not structurally exist until the dynamics gain a blocking constraint.

The fix, restated

if gap_to_car_ahead < OVERTAKE_ATTEMPT_GAP and not overtake_succeeded: lap_time = max(own_lap_time, car_ahead_lap_time + dirty_air_penalty(gap))

One line, in the lap-time model that Phase 8's cars actually race on. Once a trailing car within range of the car ahead is capped at that car's pace (plus the dirty-air cost) unless it has actually overtaken, being behind carries a real, ongoing cost that only an overtake ends. Pitting before a rival to force them into exactly that situation then becomes a strategy with something to gain, not merely a synonym for having a faster car.

Which file that is: not only Phase 6's

It is natural to read "add it to Phase 6" as the whole fix, and earlier versions of this page said so. But the plan's F1PettingZooEnv does not use Phase 6's F1MultiCarEnv. It builds one independent single-car F1StrategyEnv per car, steps each on its own, adds up each car's own lap times and sorts the totals. No car's lap time ever depends on another car's: attempt_overtake is imported and never called, and dirty air is subtracted from the reward but not from the lap time (audit E3, again). Adding the rule to F1MultiCarEnv is right for Phase 6 and changes nothing here. For Phase 8, the rule has to go into F1PettingZooEnv.step, or the env has to be rebuilt on F1MultiCarEnv so it inherits the rule.

Reading the real plan's own Prerequisites section correctly

The real plan lists "Phase 6 complete" as a prerequisite. Per this page's argument, that line should be read as "Phase 6 complete and this blocking constraint added," not merely "Phase 6's files exist and its own listed tests pass" — Phase 6's originally-listed tests, worked through on Phase 6 page 10 (assumed background), don't check for this constraint at all, so passing them is no evidence it's present. Confirm the blocking-model test from Phase 6's Definition of Done actually passes, and then that the same test passes on F1PettingZooEnv itself, before starting any of this phase's training runs.
