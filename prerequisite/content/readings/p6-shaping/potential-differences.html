Compute a difference across each transition

Choose a state potential Φ and define F(s,a,s′)=gamma Φ(s′)−Φ(s). Add F to the base reward; use the same gamma as the return. The potential is a function of the relevant state, not the chosen action. Store the old state’s potential before stepping and the successor’s after the complete transition.

For the route pack Φ(start)=2, Φ(via)=1 and both successful terminal potentials zero. At gamma one, DIRECT has shaping −2 and shaped return 1. VIA+FINISH has shaping −1 then −1, giving shaped rewards −1,3 and total 2. VIA+WAIT with zero failure potential gives −1,−1 and total −2. Every route shifts by −2, preserving VIA+FINISH.

For the negative-cost chain Φ(A)=5, Φ(B)=2, Φ(C)=0, shaping is −3,−2 via B and −5 direct. Base totals −4/−10 become −9/−15; the gap stays 6. A potential need not supply a positive immediate bonus to be valid.

Discount the next potential

At gamma .5 the route-pack base totals become 3,2,0; correct shaped totals 1,0,−2. Using F=Φ(s′)−Φ(s) with an otherwise discounted return breaks cancellation. Recompute from the formula instead of carrying gamma-one conclusions into another task.

import math

def shaped_return(rewards, potentials, gamma, shaping_gamma=None):
    sg=gamma if shaping_gamma is None else shaping_gamma
    if len(potentials)!=len(rewards)+1 or any(not math.isfinite(v) for v in [*rewards,*potentials,gamma,sg]) or not 0<=gamma<=1 or not 0<=sg<=1:raise ValueError("finite rewards/potentials and valid discounts required")
    return sum(gamma**i*(r+sg*potentials[i+1]-potentials[i]) for i,r in enumerate(rewards))

Recap and self-check

Is a flat bonus whenever position is better than last a potential difference?

No. It does not subtract the previous state’s potential or apply the task discount to its successor.
