The env and the loop

F1PettingZooEnv is page 04's Parallel API: possible_agents = ["agent_0", …], a 19-number observation per agent (Phase 6's, recomputed per car by _extend_obs), and per-agent dicts from reset() and step(actions). Two lines of its step() repeat earlier findings. base_reward = -lap_times[agent] - dirty_air: the first term is the raw, uncentred lap time (audit Finding C, which Phase 9 measures), and the second charges the wake to the reward but not the lap time (E3). And each car runs in its own F1StrategyEnv, so no car affects another (page 06).

The loop is page 01's independent learners: one DuelingDoubleDQNAgent as agent_0, the only one that stores, updates and decays ε; every other car is a frozen snapshot from the pool, re-drawn each episode, played greedily.

Neither gives wrong results; both make the run far slower than it needs to be.

Case 6 · the verdict

A sampler that can't sample, in a race that can't show the result

Why does the run die? The pool sampler slices an empty "newest 20%" while the pool holds its one seed checkpoint, and np.random.choice fails on 70% of draws. The seed stays alone until episode 1,000, so no run gets there. Could its histogram be trusted if it lived? Not as planned. The seed opponent is a fixed script (23 of 30 untrained networks repeat one call). The env lets no car affect another, so the undercut the histogram looks for can't exist; in the duel with blocking off every delta is 0. And with blocking in, one run's histogram is weak evidence: a plain shuffle calls a shared safety car a reaction, and four seeds of one setup spread from 9% to 78%.

What should the plan change? The prerequisites don't edit the plan; these go to whoever implements Phase 8.

1. Make it run. In CheckpointPool.sample, a pool with no newest slice samples from what it has, with a test at n = 1. Seed the pool with a stochastic opponent or Phase 3's rule-based agents, not a greedy untrained network.

2. Make the result possible. Put page 06's rule in F1PettingZooEnv.step, or build the env on F1MultiCarEnv, with the wake in the lap time (E3) and a centred reward (Finding C). Read the prerequisite "Phase 6 complete" as "and the stuck-car test passes on this env".

3. Make the result evidence. Report the histogram beside a null shuffled only among races that share a scenario seed, beside a blocking-off run, and over several training seeds.

Also for the implementer: build the env once outside the episode loop, and cache opponents by checkpoint path.
