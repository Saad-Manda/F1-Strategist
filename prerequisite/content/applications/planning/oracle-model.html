Build the model that the benchmark actually solves

The Phase 3.5 project plan, phase-3.5-dp-oracle.md, prepares a finite dynamic-programming reference for the racing environment. Its model must share the environment's timing, pit ordering, eligibility and physical units. This reading specifies preparation; it does not claim the production oracle is implemented or verified.

The internal state can be a hashable tuple: lap, compound, tyre age, used-dry-compound mask, current safety-car flag and current weather. Lap gives the finite deadline. Compound and age determine pace, wear and warmup. The used mask distinguishes histories relevant to the two-compound requirement. SC and weather determine current costs and successor distributions.

Enumerate ranges with their assumptions

For an illustrative 55-lap race, five compounds, ages 1–55, eight usage masks, two SC flags and three weather regimes, the rectangular count is:

states = 55 * 5 * 55 * 8 * 2 * 3
entries = states * 6
assert states == 726000
assert entries == 4356000

The old text called this product 363,000; that omitted a factor of two. Without a weather dimension, the count is 242,000. Values and action tables, outcome branching, array dtype and indexing overhead add separate memory/work costs. Runtime targets such as ten seconds must be measured on a declared machine and input, not inferred from the word “finite.”

Many rectangular combinations are unreachable: age cannot exceed the current lap from a fresh start; a fitted slick implies a matching usage bit. A forward reachability pass can prune cells before solving. Do not promise a fixed pruning factor without measurement, and do not prune states needed by supported starting-compound options.

State sufficiency and exogenous processes

Oracle B uses a fitted weather row conditional on current regime. SC uses a green-start hazard and, when active, a continuation probability derived from its duration law. Multiplying next SC and weather probabilities assumes conditional independence. Non-memoryless durations may require elapsed-duration state; template weather may require a hidden scenario/history description. Those issues do not disappear when tuples replace neural observations.

Use the shared compute_action_mask. Timing uses the old tyre for the current lap, with flag-dependent pit loss, then updates compound, age, usage and lap for the next decision. Keep current-state and successor-state information distinct. Terminal continuation is zero; illegal actions are excluded before minimizing.

When disabling noise gives an expectation

If lap time is deterministic_cost(s,a)+noise and noise is additively zero-mean conditional on s,a, its expected immediate cost is deterministic_cost. Calling the shared timing function with noise disabled then computes that expectation exactly. This requires more than an unconditional zero mean.

If noise changes next state, legal masks or ending flags, its effect also enters the successor distribution. If timing clips the sum at a lower bound, E[max(bound,c+noise)] need not equal max(bound,c). For example, c=0, bound=0 and equally likely noise ±1 give expected clipped cost 0.5 although zero-noise cost is 0. Treat these as different models, not interchangeable shortcuts.

Recap and self-check

Can copying a convenient lap-time formula into the planner certify model agreement?

No. Share production dynamics to avoid drift, then independently verify selected physical examples. A copied formula can diverge silently; a shared erroneous function still needs witnesses.
