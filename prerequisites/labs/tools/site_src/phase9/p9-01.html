Warm-up, in the page · one REINFORCE step by hand

Three actions with logits z = (0.5, 0, −1). The middle action is sampled, the return from that step is G = −3, and the step size is α = 0.2. Give each answer to four decimals (±0.005 is accepted).

The gradient of log π(middle) with respect to the logits is the one-hot vector of the sampled action minus the probabilities.

Now the update z ← z + α · G · ∇ log π(middle).

What did the update do to the action that was taken?
Worked solution for the warm-up

Probabilities 0.5465, 0.3315, 0.1220. Log-gradient of the middle action: (−0.5465, 0.6685, −0.1220), which adds up to 0. With α · G = −0.6 the new logits are (0.8279, −0.4011, −0.9268): the middle action drops from 0.33 to about 0.21.
