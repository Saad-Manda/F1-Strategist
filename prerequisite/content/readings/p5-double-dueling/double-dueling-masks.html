Apply masks to decisions, not to the raw architecture

The unchanged dueling pack centers over all representable actions. Compute its finite Q vector first. Then current legal actions constrain exploration and exploitation; successor legal actions constrain the target's selection. Centering only over legal advantages defines a different architecture whose common level can change when the mask changes.

Putting −infinity into raw advantages before computing their mean corrupts the forward values: subtracting an infinite mean can produce infinities or NaN. Keep finite raw outputs and use masked_fill only on the array used for argmax/max. Illegal values can still affect the shared centered representation, but are never executable candidates.

Check negative values, exact ties and endings

For online [−5,−4,100] with legal [true,true,false], the legal winner is action 1. Multiplying the illegal entry by zero gives a zero that wrongly beats both legal negatives. Filtering or −infinity masking avoids this. For tied legal online values [5,5], argmax selects the first under the taught target contract; different target values make this convention observable.

A terminal successor has no decision and may have an all-false mask. Return its reward without selecting an action or multiplying an invalid maximum by zero. An ongoing or truncated successor needs at least one legal action, even at gamma zero under these pack contracts. If both terminated and truncated are true, termination removes continuation.

Current actions in replay should have passed the current mask when collected. The next mask must correspond to the final successor observation, not the current state or the next episode's reset. If observation and mask disagree, correct their contract before interpreting target losses.

Build independent witnesses

  1. Use the supplied 1.5 Double versus 4.5 vanilla example; raise the illegal estimates without changing either answer.
  2. Check a terminal row with no legal successor and a nonterminal all-invalid row that must fail.
  3. Check first-index ties, batch size one, boolean mask dtype and matching [B,A] shapes.
  4. Verify target tensors have no gradient connection, even if their inputs are trainable.
  5. Verify adding a constant to every raw advantage leaves combined Q unchanged, and that effective deviations average zero.

A comparison curve cannot substitute for these small tests; a learner can sometimes earn reward despite a mask bug.
