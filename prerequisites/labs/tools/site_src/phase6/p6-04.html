3 · Which curve was the docstring describing?

The page showed that k = 5 gives 92.4% at 1.0 s, not the claimed 80%. Move the slider until the curve passes through the 80% dot, then confirm it with algebra: solve 1 / (1 + e^(−0.5k)) = 0.8.

4 · Reading the model

A car 0.3 s a lap faster on its own sits 0.3 s behind, inside the wake. Its lap time is taken to include the wake loss. How likely is a pass this lap?
You want passing to be harder without changing which cars may attempt it. Which constant do you change?
Worked solution

Wake: 0.4 s at 0.3 s, 0.1 s at 1.2 s. Pass at 0.8 s faster: 1 / (1 + e^−1.5) = 81.8%; with three laps following, 81.8% + 20% capped to 95%. The docstring's 80% at 1.0 s needs k = 2 ln 4 = 2.77, which gives 94.1% at 1.5 s. A 0.3 s faster car in a 0.4 s wake is not faster this lap, so it gets no attempt. Steepness changes how often, the attempt gap changes who.
