Worked solution

1. 55 × 5 × 55 × 8 × 2 × 3 = 726,000 states. Reachable (lap, age) pairs: 1 + 2 + … + 55 = 1,540 of 3,025.

2. Twice the laps: × 4. Five weather states: × 5/3. Eight compounds: × 8/5. Fuel to 0.1 kg: × 1,101. The fuel load does the most damage by far. With the gap feature alone: 726,000 × 601 = 436,326,000 states, × 6 actions × 8 bytes ≈ 20.9 GB.

3. Planning: backward induction, scoring every sequence with the formula, value iteration. Learning: Q-learning, DQN.
