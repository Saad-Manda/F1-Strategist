The plan's config: n_steps 256 per environment, 4 environments, batch_size 64, n_epochs 10.

How many gradient steps does one update take?

A vector of environments

SB3 trains on a VecEnv: n_envs copies stepped together, one call returning stacked observations, rewards and done flags. It follows from page 04: PPO needs a fresh batch before every update, and parallel copies collect one quickly. DummyVecEnv steps the copies in turn in one process; SubprocVecEnv gives each its own process, which pays only when one step is expensive. Page 07 settles which this race needs.

transitions per update = n_steps × n_envs = 256 × 4 = 1,024 minibatches = 1,024 / batch_size = 1,024 / 64 = 16 gradient steps = 16 × n_epochs = 16 × 10 = 160

batch_size should divide 1,024. Nothing enforces it: plain PPO warns once and trains on a short last minibatch; MaskablePPO does the same silently (lab p9-06, part E).

.learn(), .predict() and the mask

model.learn(total_timesteps) alternates a rollout (run the policy for n_steps in every copy, storing observations, calls, rewards, log-probabilities and values) and an update (GAE over the rollout, then n_epochs of clipped steps over shuffled minibatches). Pages 01 to 04 are inside that one call. model.predict(obs, deterministic=True) acts: the most likely call, no sampling.

Masking is Phase 1's idea with a new mechanism: wrap the env in ActionMasker(env, mask_fn), and MaskablePPO sets illegal calls' probability to zero before sampling, so the ratio of page 04 is always computed over legal calls only. tensorboard_log=... logs reward, losses, entropy, clip_fraction, approx_kl and explained_variance.

The numbers say160

1,024 transitions make 16 minibatches of 64, and each is used once per pass for 10 passes: 160 gradient steps, then a new rollout. Every one of those steps also updates the critic.

How far each step can move the critic is what decides the case.

The critic's pace

SB3's critic is a network trained by Adam, at the plan's learning rate of 3 × 10−4. Adam divides each step by the gradient's recent size, so a critic ten times further from the truth doesn't take steps ten times as big. Measured, MaskablePPO on the toy race: after 500 races the critic's value of lap 1 was −14.6 with the raw reward and −14.7 centred. It moves at the same pace either way: 9.9 every 10,000 steps.

What differs is the distance. The plan the learner starts on returns −63 centred and −263 raw. Centred, the critic is close enough by race 500, the plan starts improving, and it is within 1 s by race 700 to 900. Raw, after 2,000 races the critic is at −44.9 and the policy hasn't moved: explained_variance is 0.0 (the critic explains none of the returns), and the advantages that drive the policy are mostly the critic's error, the same whatever call was made. Left to run on, the same raw run starts improving at 70,000 steps and is first within 1 s at 130,000: about eight times as long.

Two fixes, both measured on the same three seeds. Centre the reward (Finding C, Phase 1's reward_offset): 0.00 s by race 2,000. Or keep it raw and wrap the env in VecNormalize(norm_reward=True), the plan's own fallback: it divides each reward by the running return's spread, about 61 here, so the critic's target is about −3.7. Also 0.00 s by race 2,000. Pick raw, through VecNormalize above to see it.

Deep diveWhy the table critics didn't have this problem160 words

Pages 02 and 03's critics are tables updated by V ← V + 0.1 (G − V): each step is a tenth of the error, so a target 263 away is reached as fast, in relative terms, as one 63 away. Adam's step is set by its learning rate, not by the error, which is what makes it robust to badly scaled gradients and also what makes it slow to cover a large distance. SB3 also clips the gradient's norm at 0.5 by default (the plan's max_grad_norm), which caps the step for the same reason.

The real race is further still. Its laps are near 90 s and there are 52 of them, so an uncentred return is about −4,700, eighteen times the toy race's −263. The toy race doesn't say how many steps that would need with the plan's [128, 128] network; it says the critic's pace, not its target, sets the wait.

CrackedThe library's critic moves at a fixed pace, 9.9 every 10,000 steps here, whatever its error. With the raw reward it has four times as far to go as centred, and the policy waits for it: 38.48 s at 2,000 races, within 1 s only after 130,000 steps. Centring or VecNormalize: 0.00 s by 2,000 races.
